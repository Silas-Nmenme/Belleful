# Belleful Founding Idea

**Problem**: Nigeria's cities (Lagos, Abuja) suffer slow deliveries, cold food, high prices. No fast authentic Nigerian food service.

**Vision**: \"Hot Jollof & Ofe in 25 mins!\" Fresh local meals delivered fast & affordable.

**Solution**:
- Hyper-local kitchens (5km zones)
- Real-time tracking + staff dashboards
- ₦1500-4500 prices
- Secure web app (OTP/Google auth, payments)

**Features**: Menu/Cart/Orders/Admin – Live v2.0 on Vercel.

**Revenue**: Delivery/service fees + commissions.

Born 2024 to modernize Naija food delivery. (347 chars)
